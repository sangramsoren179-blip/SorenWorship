# SorenWorship

A Christian Song Lyrics App.

## About

SorenWorship is a Christian song lyrics app designed to provide a simple and accessible way to discover and read Christian songs.

The project aims to bring song lyrics and relevant video links together in one place, making it easier for users to read, watch, and listen to Christian songs.

## Planned Features

- **Language Selection:** Allow users to explore Christian songs by language.
- **Browse Songs:** Explore available Christian songs.
- **Search Songs:** Find songs by title or keywords.
- **Read Lyrics:** Access song lyrics in a clean and readable format.
- **Watch & Listen:** Watch and listen to songs through video links in the browser.
- **Multiple Languages:** Support additional languages in the future.
- **Reading Customization:** Adjust text size and other reading preferences.
- **Favorites:** Save favourite songs for quick access.
